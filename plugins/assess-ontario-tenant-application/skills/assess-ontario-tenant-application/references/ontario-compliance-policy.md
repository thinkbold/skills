# Ontario Compliance Policy Snapshot

Version: `2026.08.03`

Last reviewed: `2026-08-03`

Reviewer role: `qualified-ontario-reviewer`

This is an operational policy snapshot, not legal advice. A qualified Ontario reviewer must review it at least every 90 days. An expired snapshot allows extraction but blocks a completed recommendation. Legal changes are never applied automatically.

## Scope

Use only for ordinary market-rate residential housing in Ontario where the applicant does not share a kitchen or bathroom with the owner or the owner's family. Exclude RGI/social-housing eligibility, commercial leases, existing-tenant monitoring, and every cross-case tenant list.

Apply a uniform evidence checklist. Assess applications individually. Do not rank people, predict default, make automated tenancy decisions, use protected characteristics, or substitute an open-web search for an official police record check.

Collect, use, disclose, and retain only evidence needed for the stated rental-screening purpose. The deployer must provide a privacy contact and a process for applicant access, correction, withdrawal where applicable, and deletion requests.

## Retention

Plan deletion of raw identity, credit, income, bank, and working files 30 days after decision or withdrawal. Plan deletion of derived case artifacts after 13 calendar months. A documented legal hold pauses affected deletion. The deletion tool is dry-run by default.
