# Public-Source Policy

## Authorization and Purpose

General authorization must disclose open-web categories and the narrow identity/fact-verification purpose. Facebook and LinkedIn each require separate, informed, revocable platform consent.

Refusal, withdrawal, a private account, no matching account, or inability to search is neutral. Record the outcome in the public appendix, never as a core limitation.

## Whitelist

- Government and official registries.
- Courts, tribunals, CanLII, LTB, and primary legal sources.
- Corporate and professional licence registries.
- Applicant or organization official sites.
- News sources with editorial accountability and correction processes.
- Facebook and LinkedIn under separate current platform consent.

Exclude people-search services, data brokers, tenant blacklists, anonymous forums, leaks, unattributed reposts, and personal accusations.

## Identity Gate

Do not display a same-name result until at least two independent non-protected identifiers match, such as full name plus verified historical city or full name plus verified employer. Do not use protected characteristics as an identifier.

Record source type, URL, title, access or publication date, matching basis, and only the minimal relevant excerpt. Do not store a full page or screenshot by default.

## LTB and Legal Records

List every identity-matched LTB record in the appendix without sorting applicants or changing any core state. State party role, case type, status, result, and whether the decision made a finding against the applicant. Appearance in a proceeding is not a negative fact.

News or legal sources may mention alleged or adjudicated criminal conduct. Report only the source fact after the identity gate. Use this disclaimer: `Not an official criminal record check; not used in evidence states or recommendations.` Never make a criminal-record conclusion.

## Facebook and LinkedIn

Permitted fields are limited to accessible-account existence, displayed-name consistency, declared LinkedIn employer or professional-fact consistency, and non-content metadata showing the account predates the application.

Do not inspect or retain posts, photos, friends, connections, likes, political or religious views, family relationships, lifestyle, or location history. Remove protected information. On withdrawal, delete collected social content and retain only the withdrawal audit event.

## Isolation

Public-source identifiers, URLs, excerpts, outcomes, and findings belong only in public work artifacts and `public-records.md`. They must never enter core facts, financial calculations, discrepancies, evidence states, recommendations, or human reason selection.
